Getting Started

Welcome to Your Routine! This application helps you organize your daily activities and track your personal development across different life areas.

Key Features:
  • Schedule Management: Add, edit, and organize your daily activities
  • Life Balance Assessment: Evaluate your time and satisfaction across different life areas
  • Personal Development Tracking: Monitor your progress and get personalized suggestions
  • Multilingual Support: Available in English and Portuguese
How to Use
Adding Activities:
  1. Click the "Add Event" button in the Schedule view
  2. Fill in the activity details (title, category, description)
  3. Select the days and time for the activity
  4. Click "Add" to save
Managing Activities:
  • Click on any activity to edit it
  • Drag activities to different time slots to reschedule
  • Use different colors for different categories
Self-Assessment:
  1. Go to the Dashboard
  2. Use the sliders to rate your time and satisfaction for each area
  3. Select your focus areas
  4. Save your assessment to get personalized suggestions
Tips for Success
  • Start with a few key activities and gradually build your schedule
  • Be honest in your self-assessment to get meaningful suggestions
  • Regularly review and adjust your schedule based on your needs
  • Use the color coding to quickly identify different types of activities
